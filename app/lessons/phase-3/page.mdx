# Lesson 3: 4x4 vs 6-Value Representation

This lesson resolves a common confusion: "Are we using a 4x4 matrix or a 6D vector?"

Answer: both, for different jobs.

- The `4x4` tensor form is a transformation structure.
- The `6-value` form is the unique field content.

## Learning goals

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to:

- Explain why these forms are compatible, not contradictory.
- Identify why only six tensor entries are independent.
- Choose the right representation for UI/storage vs transform operations.

## The structure-content distinction

Think of two layers:

- Structure layer (`4x4`): where spacetime-compatible transform math is applied.
- Content layer (`Ex,Ey,Ez,Bx,By,Bz`): the compressed state you keep and display.

The tensor is antisymmetric, so many positions are zero or mirrored. That is why six values are sufficient to carry unique information.

## Why not store full 4x4 all the time?

You can, but it is inefficient for this educational app:

- More storage than needed for unique state.
- Harder to read for learners.
- Adds noise to architecture comparison where we care about representation cost.

## Recommended practice (used in this site)

1. Keep canonical state in six-value form.
2. Lift to `4x4` only when applying frame/transform logic.
3. Extract back to six-value form for display and downstream simulators.

## Beginner lens

Six numbers are the ingredients. `4x4` is the mixing bowl.

## Student lens

Convert to tensor for transform math, then convert back for intuitive interpretation.

## Advanced lens

Treat this as a serialization strategy around a transform algebra: minimal payload, explicit lifting/lowering steps.

## Common misconception

"If tensor is 4x4, there must be 16 independent values."

No. Antisymmetry constraints reduce independent content to six.

## Inline Diagram

```text
Antisymmetric tensor pattern (conceptual):

      0    Ex   Ey   Ez
    -Ex    0   -Bz   By
    -Ey   Bz    0   -Bx
    -Ez  -By   Bx    0

Independent slots: Ex, Ey, Ez, Bx, By, Bz  (6 total)
All other entries are zero-diagonal or mirrored sign entries.
```
